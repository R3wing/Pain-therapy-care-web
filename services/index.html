Advanced Technology

Non-invasive, drug-free treatments that use advanced technology to help reduce pain and improve movement.

Shockwave Therapy

A non-invasive, drug-free treatment for chronic musculoskeletal pain.

  • Non-invasive
  • Drug-free
Learn more about Shockwave Therapy

EMTT Therapy

A non-operative, drug-free technology that uses focused electromagnetic energy to reach deep-tissue pain.

  • Non-surgical
  • Drug-free
Learn more about EMTT Therapy

Laser Therapy

Low-level laser light reaches deep tissue to support healing and ease musculoskeletal pain, without incisions or anesthesia.

  • Non-invasive
  • No anesthesia
Learn more about Laser Therapy

PIT Therapy

Small dextrose injections near an irritated nerve to support natural healing and ease nerve-related pain.

  • Non-surgical
  • Drug-free
Learn more about PIT Therapy

TECAR Therapy

TECAR (Transfer of Energy Capacitive and Resistive) is a radiofrequency heat therapy that encourages healing in joints and muscles.

  • Non-invasive
  • Drug-free
Learn more about TECAR Therapy

Cryotherapy

Localized cold therapy applies extreme cold to the painful area to help ease pain and swelling, without surgery or medication.

  • Non-surgical
  • Non-pharmacological
Learn more about Cryotherapy

View Advanced Technology

Injections

Targeted injections, used when clinically appropriate, to help relieve pain and inflammation.

Steroid Injection

A minimally invasive option that aims to bring fast relief at the source of your pain.

  • Minimally invasive
  • X-ray (fluoroscopy) guided
Learn more about Steroid Injection

HA Knee Injection

Hyaluronic acid adds lubrication to the knee joint to ease arthritis pain.

  • Ultrasound guided
  • Minimally invasive
Learn more about HA Knee Injection

Ultrasound Guided Injection

Live ultrasound imaging guides the needle to the exact source of your pain: a minimally invasive approach instead of a hit-or-miss one.

  • Live ultrasound imaging
  • Outpatient procedure
Learn more about Ultrasound Guided Injection

View Injections

Rehab & Therapy

Hands-on therapy programs that help you rebuild strength, mobility and independence.

Physical Therapy & OT

Physical therapy restores movement and strength.

  • Non-invasive
  • Home exercise program
Learn more about Physical Therapy & OT

Aqua Therapy

Therapeutic exercise in water lets you move without being limited by pain, supporting recovery from injury, surgery and chronic pain.

  • Non-invasive
  • Guided by trained therapists
Learn more about Aqua Therapy

IMS Therapy

A non-surgical treatment for chronic pain and musculoskeletal problems linked to nerve dysfunction and muscle tightness, using fine acupuncture-like needles.

  • Non-surgical
  • Fine, acupuncture-like needles
Learn more about IMS Therapy

TMJ Therapy

The temporomandibular joints connect your jaw to your skull.

  • Treatment based on cause and severity
  • Medicare & Medicaid accepted
Learn more about TMJ Therapy

Pelvic Floor Therapy

Hands-on rehabilitation combined with shockwave, EMTT and laser therapy for a tight, painful pelvic floor.

  • Hands-on rehabilitation
  • For women and men
Learn more about Pelvic Floor Therapy

Questions? We have answers.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

Your provider will evaluate your pain and recommend the options that fit your situation. Request an appointment and tell us where it hurts.

Do you accept Medicare and Medicaid?

Yes. We accept Medicare, Medicaid and most major insurance plans. Check your coverage on our insurance page.

Where can I get these treatments?

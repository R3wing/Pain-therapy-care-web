At Pain Therapy Medical Care, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal data. By using our website and services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Personal Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information from our users:

  • Name
  • Phone Number

This information is collected when you voluntarily provide it, for example, when subscribing to our services or contacting us.

When you use the forms on our website, we may also collect your email address, preferred location, preferred time to be contacted, insurance plan type and plan name, the general area where you feel pain, whether you need help with transportation, and your text messaging (SMS) consent preferences.

2. Use of Personal Information

The personal information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and improving our services.
  • Communicating with you, responding to your inquiries, and providing support.
  • Personalizing your experience on our website.
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing Personal Information

At Pain Therapy Medical Care, we are committed to never sharing your personal information with third parties or affiliated companies for marketing purposes. However, under certain circumstances, we may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third parties that help us operate our website and provide our services (for example, technology providers or communication platforms). These providers are required to protect your information and may only use it for the specific purposes for which they were contracted.
  • Legal Requirements: When necessary to comply with laws, regulations, legal processes, or government requests.

4. SMS Subscriptions

If you subscribe to our SMS services, please be advised that:

  • Your phone number will not be shared with third parties or affiliated companies for marketing purposes.
  • We will use your phone number exclusively to send you information related to our services and relevant updates.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent collected as part of the SMS consent process will not be shared with any third parties.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. See our Terms and Conditions.

5. Information Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no security system is foolproof, so we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

6. Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies, including Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use our website and to improve it. These tools collect information such as pages visited, device type and general location. We do not send your name, phone number, email or health information to analytics tools. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

7. Health Information

Information you submit through our website forms is used to respond to you and schedule your visit. Please do not include detailed medical information in our contact form. Medical information we receive as your healthcare provider is protected under HIPAA and described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

8. Third-Party Services

Our website uses third-party services to operate, including website hosting, form and messaging providers, map services (OpenStreetMap map tiles and links to Google Maps), and analytics. These providers may process limited technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver their services.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Object to the use of your information for certain purposes.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, when the processing of your data is based on your consent.

To exercise these rights, you can contact us using the information provided in the contact section of this page.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the date of the last update. We recommend that you periodically review this policy to be informed about how we protect your information.

New Patients: What to Expect at Pain Therapy Medical Care

Living with pain is hard enough. Getting care should not be. This page covers what we treat, how your first visit works, what to bring, where we are, and how to book. If you are reading this for a parent, spouse or friend, feel free to send them the link.

What we treat

We help people with ongoing and recent pain, including:

  • Lower back pain
  • Sciatica
  • Herniated disc
  • Neck pain
  • Pinched nerve
  • Knee pain
  • Hip pain
  • Arthritis
  • Shoulder and rotator cuff
  • Peripheral neuropathy
  • Plantar fasciitis
  • Carpal tunnel
  • TMJ pain
  • Spinal stenosis
  • Chronic pain
  • Pelvic floor pain

How we treat pain

Our approach starts with non-surgical options. Depending on your evaluation, your plan may include:

  • Technology-based treatments

    • Shockwave therapy
    • EMTT
    • Laser therapy
    • TECAR therapy

  • Injections

    • Gel (HA) knee injections
    • Ultrasound-guided injections
    • Steroid injections

  • Therapy services

    • Physical and occupational therapy
    • Aqua therapy
    • Pelvic floor therapy

Your first visit, step by step

  1. 1

    Book

    Call the office closest to you or request an appointment online. Same-day appointments are often available.

  2. 2

    Check in

    The front desk confirms your details and insurance.

  3. 3

    Evaluation

    A provider asks about your pain, reviews your history and any imaging, and examines the area that hurts.

  4. 4

    Your plan

    The provider explains what is likely causing your pain and the treatment options that fit your case, so you can decide together.

What to bring

  • Photo ID
  • Your insurance card(s)
  • A referral, if your doctor gave you one
  • Any recent x-rays, MRIs or reports
  • A list of the medications you take

Insurance

We accept Medicaid, Medicare and most major insurance plans, including Healthfirst, MetroPlus and Fidelis. If you are not sure your plan is covered, call the office before your visit and we will help you check.

Common questions

Can I be seen the same day?

Same-day appointments are often available. Call the office you want to visit to check today's openings.

How long is the first visit?

Plan for check-in plus the evaluation. Your provider will take the time to explain your options before any treatment starts.

Can someone come with me?

Yes. Many patients bring a family member to help with questions and directions.

Do you speak my language?

Our Williamsburg, Flatbush and Inwood offices support English, Spanish and Yiddish. Monsey supports English and Yiddish. [CONFIRM: languages spoken at Crown Heights]

What if I have already tried physical therapy or injections elsewhere?

Bring what you have, including reports and imaging. Knowing what you have tried helps your provider suggest what to try next.

Do I need to bring my x-rays or MRI?

If you have them, yes. They help your provider understand your pain. If you do not have imaging, your provider will tell you whether you need it.

Can you help me get to my appointment?

Yes. We can arrange transportation to your appointment, depending on your insurance plan. Please request it at least one day before your visit and call to confirm your ride.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

Yes, we accept walk-ins. Patients with a scheduled appointment are seen first, so booking ahead is the best way to avoid a wait.

Do you complete disability or other forms?

No. We do not complete paperwork such as disability forms.

Ready for your first visit?

Same-day appointments are often available.

Important facts about chronic pain

Understanding the reality of chronic pain is the first step toward effective treatment and recovery.

  • Complex diagnosis

    Chronic pain is not like other serious medical conditions. Its causes can be difficult to diagnose with conventional medical tests.

  • Often undertreated

    Recurring pain is often left undertreated.

  • Pain is real

    While each person’s experience of pain is specific to them, it is virtually never “only in your head.”

The impact of untreated pain

  • Long-term disability

    Unrelieved pain is a better predictor for long-term disability than almost any other factor.

  • Silent suffering

    People are often scared to admit the extent of their suffering. However, those who stay silent generally fail to receive effective treatment.

  • Quality of life

    Refusing to acknowledge your pain results in severe physiological and psychological outcomes that diminish your quality of life. Chronic pain leaves many of its victims solitary and discouraged.

Questions? We have answers.

Do you accept Medicare and Medicaid?

Yes. We accept Medicare, Medicaid and most major insurance plans, including for Shockwave, EMTT and Laser Therapy. Call us and we will review your plan with you before your first visit.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

Yes, we accept walk-ins. Patients with a scheduled appointment are seen first, so booking ahead is the best way to avoid a wait.

Can you help me get to my appointment?

Yes. We can arrange transportation to your appointment, depending on your insurance plan. Please request it at least one day before your visit and call to confirm your ride.

Cold Laser Pain Therapy in Brooklyn, NYC

Low-level laser light reaches deep tissue to support healing and ease musculoskeletal pain, without incisions or anesthesia.

  • Non-invasive
  • No anesthesia
  • Medicare & Medicaid accepted

What is cold laser therapy?

Cold laser therapy, also called low-level laser therapy (LLLT), uses light at specific wavelengths to reach deep tissues such as muscle, tendon and ligament. It is used for chronic and acute musculoskeletal pain, and for some patients it is a non-intrusive alternative to injections and surgery.

After assessing the area, your provider selects the wavelength and output of the device. Dr. Henry Sardar offers this treatment, which is also part of our pelvic floor therapy.

How it may help

  • Less inflammation

    Increased blood flow and less swelling.

  • Pain blocking

    Helps block pain signals.

  • Cell healing

    Supports cellular healing and regeneration.

Who can benefit?

Cold laser therapy may be right for you if you have:

  • Pain months after surgery
  • Recurring pain from an old injury
  • Nagging joint or muscle pain
  • Chronic pain that has not responded to other therapies
  • Joint flexibility issues

Your physician decides whether this treatment is right for you.

Conditions treated

  • Painful muscle spasms
  • Knee pain
  • Low back pain
  • Neck pain
  • Tennis elbow
  • Bursitis
  • Tendonitis
  • Muscle strains

What to expect

A session lasts up to 10 minutes. Multiple sessions may be needed for the best results.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Your provider assesses the painful area.

  2. 02

    Settings

    The wavelength and output of the laser are selected for your condition.

  3. 03

    Treatment

    You may feel mild warmth and possibly some tingling. There are no incisions and no anesthesia.

What if cold laser therapy doesn't work for me?

We design a tailored plan for each patient. Cold laser therapy can be combined with:

  • Shockwave therapy
  • Cryotherapy
  • EMTT
  • TECAR therapy
  • Aqua therapy
  • Kinesiology taping
  • Ultrasound-guided injections
  • Fluoroscopy-guided epidural and facet injections
  • Physical and occupational therapy

Questions about this treatment

Is cold laser therapy painful?

No. You may feel mild warmth and possibly some tingling. A session takes up to 10 minutes, with no incisions and no anesthesia.

How many sessions will I need?

Multiple sessions may be needed for the best results. Your provider will recommend a plan after assessing you.

Is cold laser the same as low-level laser therapy (LLLT)?

Yes. Cold laser therapy is also called low-level laser therapy.

Is cold laser therapy covered by insurance?

We accept Medicare, Medicaid and most major plans. Call your nearest location and we will review your coverage for this treatment.

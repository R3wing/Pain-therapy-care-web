Cryotherapy for Pain Relief in Brooklyn, NYC

Localized cold therapy applies extreme cold to the painful area to help ease pain and swelling, without surgery or medication.

  • Non-surgical
  • Non-pharmacological
  • Sessions of 2 to 3 minutes
Request Appointment Call a location

What is cryotherapy?

Cryotherapy is cold therapy: extreme cold is applied locally to a painful area. It follows the same principle as an ice pack, which reduces swelling and pain, applied on a larger scale.

The cold is applied with a device called Cryo Polar Bear, at temperatures between -148°F and -220°F in a localized area. Dr. Henry Sardar offers it to all patients with acute or chronic pain, not only athletes.

How it may help

  • Pain relief

    Cold numbs the area and helps reduce pain signals.

  • Less swelling

    Cold constricts blood vessels to reduce swelling.

  • Circulation

    Afterwards, blood vessels widen and bring more circulation to the tissue.

Conditions treated

  • Neck pain
  • Low back pain
  • Post-orthopedic surgery
  • Arthritis
  • Osteoarthritis
  • Sprains
  • Tendonitis
  • Runner's knee
  • Delayed onset muscle soreness
  • Muscle spasms
  • Neuromas or nerve entrapment (pinched nerve)

What to expect

A session takes 2 to 3 minutes, depending on your condition.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    You meet Dr. Sardar to build a treatment plan.

  2. 02

    Treatment

    The cold is applied locally to the painful area.

  3. 03

    Afterwards

    Numbness in the treated area is normal. Most patients can return to their daily activities.

Questions about this treatment

How long is a cryotherapy session?

A session takes 2 to 3 minutes, depending on your condition.

Is numbness normal?

Yes. Numbness in the treated area is normal after a session.

Can I combine cryotherapy with physical therapy?

Yes. A short session before physical therapy can reduce swelling and tenderness, which can make rehab more comfortable.

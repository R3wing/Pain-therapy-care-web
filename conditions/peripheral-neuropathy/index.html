What is peripheral neuropathy?

Peripheral neuropathy is nerve damage with many possible causes. Medications can reduce symptoms such as burning and tingling, but they are aimed at the symptoms, which is why we also look at the nerves themselves.

Common symptoms

  • Tingling in the toes
  • Numbness that creeps upward
  • Feet that feel wrapped in cotton
  • Stumbling on curbs you used to step over

What causes peripheral neuropathy?

  • Diabetes, the most common cause
  • Chemotherapy
  • Autoimmune conditions
  • Chronic alcohol use
  • Vitamin deficiencies
  • Infections
  • Compression injuries

When to see a doctor

If your neuropathy symptoms are getting worse, get evaluated as early as possible.

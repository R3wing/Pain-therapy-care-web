Nerve, Hand & Foot Pain Treatment in Brooklyn, Five Towns & Monsey

Tingling, numbness and pain in the hands and feet can come from a nerve that is compressed or damaged somewhere along its path. We look for where the nerve is affected and offer drug-free care.

Book an evaluation Call a location

Nerves, Hands & Feet conditions we treat

  • Carpal Tunnel Syndrome

    Carpal tunnel syndrome is compression of the median nerve, which can cause tingling and numbness in the hand.

    Treated with: Laser Therapy, Shockwave Therapy

  • Plantar Fasciitis

    Plantar fasciitis causes stabbing heel pain, often worst in the morning.

    Treated with: Shockwave Therapy, Laser Therapy, Physical Therapy & OT

  • Peripheral Neuropathy

    Peripheral neuropathy often starts with tingling in the toes and numbness that creeps upward.

    Treated with: Laser Therapy, Shockwave Therapy

Treatments we often use for nerve, hand & foot pain

  • Laser Therapy

    Low-level laser light reaches deep tissue to support healing and ease musculoskeletal pain, without incisions or anesthesia.

    Learn more about Laser Therapy

  • Shockwave Therapy

    A non-invasive, drug-free treatment for chronic musculoskeletal pain.

    Learn more about Shockwave Therapy

  • Physical Therapy & OT

    Physical therapy restores movement and strength.

    Learn more about Physical Therapy & OT
